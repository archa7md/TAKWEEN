# TAKWEEN v3.4 — Multi-Floor Intelligent Planning

Adds functional floor assignment, public/private/service zoning, vertical-demand scoring,
locked-floor support, stair metadata, floor distribution and vertical connectivity analysis.
API: `/projects/multi-floor-plan`.

This is a design-assistance foundation; egress, fire, accessibility, structural and Saudi
municipal checks remain REVIEW items until connected to authoritative rule sources.
